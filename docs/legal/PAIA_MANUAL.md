## 7. How to request access

1. Complete the prescribed **PAIA request form** (Regulation form) and send it to
   the Information Officer at the address in clause 2.
2. Provide enough detail to identify the record, the right you seek to exercise
   (where relevant), the form of access, and your contact details.
3. Pay the prescribed **request fee** and any **access fee** (see clause 8).
4. We will respond within the periods prescribed by PAIA (ordinarily **30 days**,
   extendable as PAIA allows).

## 9. Grounds for refusal

Access may be refused on the grounds set out in **Chapter 4 of PAIA**, including
the mandatory protection of a third party's personal information (aligned with
POPIA), commercially sensitive information, legally privileged records, and
records whose disclosure would compromise safety or security.
