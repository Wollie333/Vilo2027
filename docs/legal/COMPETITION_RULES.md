# Competition Rules

> **DRAFT — pending legal review.** Two parts: (A) the flagship **Founding Race**
> rules, and (B) a reusable **master template** for every affiliate Campaign so
> each competition is CPA-compliant by default. Each competition is published at
> its own fixed, retained URL and every entrant's acceptance is version-stamped.
> Not legal advice. `[COUNSEL: …]` marks decisions.

South African **promotional competitions** are regulated by the **Consumer
Protection Act, 2008 (§36)** and **Regulation 11**. Each competition's rules must
be available free of charge and **retained for 3 years**. The platform already
publishes each competition at a fixed `/legal/<slug>` URL and records the version
each entrant accepted — the wording below fills that structure.

### §36 / Reg 11 compliance checklist (for the person publishing a competition)

- [ ] Promoter's full legal identity and contact stated
- [ ] Entry is free / no unlawful consideration
- [ ] Who may enter — and **employees + families excluded**
- [ ] **18+** age restriction
- [ ] Start **and** closing dates/times
- [ ] Full prize description **and Rand value**
- [ ] How winners are selected and **notified**
- [ ] How and when results are made public
- [ ] **Tax** treatment of prizes stated
- [ ] Dispute process + governing law (RSA)
- [ ] Rules **free of charge** and **retained 3 years** at the fixed URL
