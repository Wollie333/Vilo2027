## 1. Joining

- You must have a valid Wielo account, be **18 or older**, and provide accurate
  payout and, where required, tax details.
- Wielo may approve or decline any application at its discretion, and may run the
  program on an invite-only or open basis.

## 2. Promoting Wielo

You may promote Wielo products using the **unique referral link** and approved
marketing material provided to you. You may **not**:

- misrepresent Wielo, its products, pricing, or your relationship with Wielo;
- bid on Wielo brand terms in paid search, or run ads that appear to be Wielo's
  own;
- spam, or use deceptive, misleading, or unlawful promotion;
- **self-refer** or create referrals through your own or connected accounts; or
- offer unauthorised cash rebates or incentives that breach these terms.

You must clearly **disclose** that your links are affiliate links where the law or
platform rules require it. `[COUNSEL: confirm disclosure wording for CPA/ASA.]`

## 3. Attribution

- A referred customer is attributed to you for **30 days** from their click, and
  remains attributed to you **once they create an account** within that window.
- Attribution uses cookies and account linkage. If attribution rules conflict
  (e.g. multiple referrers), Wielo's recorded attribution governs.

## 4. Commission

- Commission is calculated on the **net amount** a referred customer actually pays
  Wielo for a product (**excluding VAT and before payout fees**), at the
  commission **rate set on that product** at the time of the sale.
- Some products pay commission **once**; others pay **recurring** commission for
  as long as configured. The product's configuration at the time of sale governs.
- Commission **accrues** when the referred payment succeeds and is **held** until
  the applicable **refund window** passes, after which it becomes **cleared** and
  payable.

## 5. Refunds, chargebacks, and clawback

If a sale is **refunded, reversed, or charged back**, the related commission is
**voided/clawed back** — whether it is still pending or has already cleared. Wielo
may offset clawed-back amounts against your future or current balance.

## 7. Campaigns, prizes, and competitions

Wielo may run time-bound **Campaigns** with leaderboards, commission structures,
and cash **prize pots**. Each such Campaign is a promotional competition
governed by its own **Competition Rules**, published at a fixed URL, which you
must accept to enter. In case of conflict, the Campaign's Competition Rules govern
for that Campaign. See the [Competition Rules](/legal).

## 8. Abuse, suspension, and voiding

Wielo may suspend or remove an Affiliate for breach, fraud, or abuse. Doing so may
**void pending commission** and forfeit Campaign standing. Wielo may reverse
commission generated through prohibited conduct.

## 9. Relationship

You are an independent participant. Nothing here creates employment, agency, or
partnership. You have no authority to bind Wielo or make representations on its
behalf beyond approved material.

## 10. Changes and termination

Wielo may update these terms or the commission rates prospectively; continued
participation after a change is acceptance. Either party may end participation at
any time; cleared, non-clawed-back commission earned before termination remains
payable subject to the threshold and these terms.
