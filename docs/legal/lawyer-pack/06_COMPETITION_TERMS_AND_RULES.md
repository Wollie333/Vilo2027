South African **promotional competitions** are regulated by the **Consumer
Protection Act 68 of 2008 (§36)** and **Regulation 11** to that Act. A competition's
rules must be **available free of charge** to every participant at a fixed URL and
**retained for 3 years**. `[COUNSEL: confirm whether this affiliate/trade incentive

1. **The promoter.** This competition is run by Wielo Platform (Pty) Ltd.

2. **How to enter.** Entry is **free**. To enter you must (a) be an **approved Wielo
   Affiliate/Founding Partner**, (b) have **accepted the [Affiliate Terms](/portal/affiliates)**,
   and (c) opt into the Founding Race and use your **Founding Race referral link** to
   refer Hosts. No purchase is required to enter, beyond any ordinary subscription a
   Host you refer chooses to pay in the normal course. `[COUNSEL: confirm there is no
   unlawful "consideration to enter".]`

3. **Who may enter.** Open to **approved Partners aged 18 or older**. **One entry per
   person** — a person may not enter more than once under different accounts, groups,
   or entities. Directors, members, employees, agents, and consultants of the
   promoter, and their immediate families, and anyone involved in running the
   competition, **may not enter or win**. `[COUNSEL: reconcile residency — the
   programme allows international Partners (PayPal settlement), which conflicts with a

4. **Competition period.** Opens **1 October 2026 at 00:00** and closes
   **28 February 2027 at 23:59** (South African Standard Time). Qualifying activity
   after the closing time does not count. The leaderboard is **frozen on the listings
   that are live at the moment of close**.

5. **How winners are determined (scoring).**
   - You score **one point per active, published listing** belonging to a Host you
     referred. Only currently-live listings count; each listing counts separately
     (a manager with 15 live properties scores 15).
   - Scores are a **live query recomputed nightly**, and finalised on the listings
     live at the moment of close.
   - **Tie-breaker:** the entrant who reached the tied score **earliest** ranks higher.
   - Wielo verifies every activation (address and photos may be verified before a
     listing counts). **Platform-recorded scores are determinative** and Wielo's
     decision on qualification and scoring is **final**.

6. **Prizes (total prize pot: R45,000).** All prizes are in South African Rand, paid
   in **cash**, on top of any commission earned:
   - **Placings (at close):** 1st — **R15,000** (plus the title "Wielo Founding
     Partner of the Year," named on the website); 2nd — **R7,500**; 3rd — **R5,000**.
   - **Milestone prizes (first Partner to reach each, once each):** first host live —
     **R500**; first to **10** live listings — **R2,000**; first to **25** live
     listings — **R5,000**.
   - **Monthly prizes:** **R1,000** each month (5 × R1,000) for the top **net increase
     in live listings** that month.
   - **Fast Start (non-competitive):** **R1,000** to **any** Partner who reaches
     **5 live listings within their first 30 days** — every qualifier wins.

   Prizes are not transferable or exchangeable for cash equivalents beyond the stated
   amounts. **All prizes require Wielo's verification and approval before award.**

8. **Publication of results.** The promoter may publish winners' **display names**

9. **Tax.** Cash prizes are paid **gross**. **Winners are responsible for any tax**

10. **Payment of prizes.** Monetary prizes are paid to the winner's verified payout

11. **Anti-gaming and disqualification.** The promoter may disqualify any entrant who

14. **Availability of rules.** These rules are available **free of charge** at the

15. **Governing law.** The laws of the **Republic of South Africa** apply. The

### §36 / Reg 11 compliance checklist (for whoever publishes a competition)

- [ ] Promoter's full legal identity and contact stated
- [ ] Entry is free / no unlawful consideration
- [ ] Who may enter — and **staff + families excluded**; one entry per person
- [ ] **18+** age restriction (and residency confirmed with counsel)
- [ ] Start **and** closing dates/times (SA time)
- [ ] Full prize description **and Rand value** (and total pot)
- [ ] How winners are selected, **notified**, and how ties break
- [ ] How and when results are made public
- [ ] **Tax** treatment of prizes stated
- [ ] Anti-gaming / disqualification grounds stated
- [ ] Dispute process + governing law (RSA); promoter's decisions final
- [ ] Rules **free of charge** and **retained 3 years** at the fixed URL
