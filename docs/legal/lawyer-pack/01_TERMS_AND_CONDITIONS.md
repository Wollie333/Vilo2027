## 3. What Wielo is — and what it is not

Wielo is a **direct-booking management platform** (software-as-a-service). We give
Hosts the tools to build a website, list in our directory, set prices and policies,
manage calendars and availability, take payment through their own payment accounts,
and communicate with Guests directly.

**Wielo is not a travel agent, tour operator, online travel marketplace, estate
agent, or a party to the agreement between a Host and a Guest.** The contract for
any stay or experience is concluded **directly between the Host and the Guest**.
Wielo does not own, control, inspect, endorse, or manage any Listing, and is not
responsible for the condition, legality, safety, quality, or accuracy of any
Listing or experience.

**Fees.** We charge Hosts a **flat Subscription fee** and, optionally, for add-on
products and services. **We do not take any commission or success fee on a Host's
bookings** — the Host keeps 100% of what their Guests pay them.
