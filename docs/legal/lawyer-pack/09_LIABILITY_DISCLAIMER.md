## 1. What Wielo does — and the limits of our role

Wielo provides **software and tools** that let Hosts run direct bookings — websites,
listings, calendars, policies, messaging, payment facilitation, reviews, and the
Looking-For feature. **Wielo is not a travel agent, tour operator, online travel
marketplace, estate agent, insurer, escrow agent, or a party to the agreement
between a Host and a Guest.**

- The contract for any **stay, experience, venue, or event is concluded directly
  between the Host and the Guest**, under the Host's own policies.
- Wielo does **not own, control, inspect, endorse, or manage** any Listing, property,
  or experience, and does not verify a Host's licences, insurance, or compliance.
- Wielo is **not the merchant of record for bookings** and does **not hold, pool, or
  custody** Host or Guest booking funds — Guests pay Hosts directly through the
  Host's own payment provider or by EFT (see the [Terms & Conditions](/terms)).
