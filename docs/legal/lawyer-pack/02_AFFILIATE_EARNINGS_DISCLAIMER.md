## 1. Why this disclaimer exists

Wielo pays **commission** to approved Affiliates who refer paying Hosts, and runs
competitions with **cash prizes**. Any figure we publish — a commission rate, an
example rand amount, a leaderboard, a projection, or a testimonial — is provided
for **illustration and information only**. This disclaimer explains what those
figures mean and, importantly, what they **do not promise**.

**By joining the Affiliate Program or a competition you confirm that you have read
and understood this disclaimer, and that you are not relying on any representation
of guaranteed income.**

## 2. No guarantee of income

- **Wielo does not guarantee that you will earn any commission, prize, or income at
  all.** Whether you earn anything depends entirely on your own efforts and results.
- **Any earnings example is illustrative, not a promise or a projection of your
  results.** For instance, if we show that a 60% rate on an annual Founding host of
  R4,999 works out to **R2,999.40**, that is an arithmetic illustration of a
  **single** referral at a **single** point in time — it is **not** a statement of
  what you will earn, how many referrals you will make, or whether any referral
  will occur.
- **Results are not typical and vary widely.** Individual outcomes depend on your
  audience, effort, skill, timing, market conditions, and factors outside anyone's
  control. Most participants' results differ from any example shown, and many may
  earn little or nothing.
- **Past or others' results do not predict your results.** Leaderboards, "top
  earner" figures, and testimonials describe specific people at specific times and
  are not a promise that you will do the same. `[COUNSEL: confirm testimonial /

## 3. How commission actually works (so the numbers are clear)

The following reflects the live programme. Full detail is in the
[Affiliate Terms](/portal/affiliates) and any applicable [Competition Rules](/legal).

- **Commission is only earned when a referred Host actually *pays* Wielo.** Merely
  signing someone up, or having them register a free account, earns nothing. A
  referral must convert into a **paying** Host.
- **Rates:** the standard rate is **25% (lifetime)**; referrals made through a
  qualifying **Founding Race** competition link earn **60% (lifetime)**. The rate is
  **fixed ("snapshotted") at the moment of the referral click** and does not change
  afterwards.
- **"Lifetime" means "for as long as that Host keeps paying" — not forever, and not
  a fixed total.** You earn on each successful payment a referred Host makes. If the
  Host cancels, lapses, or downgrades, you simply stop earning on them until (and
  unless) they pay again. There is **no guaranteed number of billing periods** and
  **no cap** — the total is whatever that Host actually pays, if anything.
- **Standard-rate referrals may attract an additional loyalty/tier bonus** (for
  example, a percentage uplift once your lifetime cleared earnings pass a
  threshold). This bonus applies to **standard** referrals only and **does not**
  apply to Founding Race (60%) referrals. Bonus thresholds and rates are set by
  Wielo and may change. `[COUNSEL: confirm whether to disclose the tier ladder

## 4. When and how you are paid

- Commission on a payment becomes payable **after the refund window passes**
  (currently on **day 33** after the payment clears). **No commission is paid on a
  payment that is refunded within that window.**
- Payouts are made by **EFT** once your cleared balance reaches the **minimum payout
  threshold** (currently **R1,000** `[COUNSEL/CONFIG: confirm — code default differs,
  see cover note conflict #4]`). Balances below the threshold roll forward.
- If a payment underlying already-paid commission is later refunded or reversed,
  the amount is **debited from your balance and carried forward** until future
  earnings absorb it.

## 5. Prizes are not income and are not guaranteed

- Competition **prizes are awarded only to participants who meet the published
  rules and are confirmed by Wielo**. Entering a competition does not entitle you to
  a prize.
- Prizes are paid in **South African Rand** and, unless stated otherwise, in
  **cash** only (not exchangeable). All prizes are subject to the applicable
  [Competition Rules](/legal) and to Wielo's verification and approval.

## 7. Honesty in your own promotion

If you promote Wielo, **you must not repeat, imply, or invent any earnings
guarantee, and you must not make income claims Wielo has not published.** You must
present the programme honestly and comply with the Affiliate Terms, the Competition
