"**Looking For**" lets a Guest post what they are looking for (accommodation, an
experience, a venue, or an event) so that Hosts can respond with quotes. This notice
explains what you share, who can see it, and Wielo's role — for **Guests** who post
and **Hosts** who respond.

---

## Part 1 — For Guests posting a request

### 1. What you post, and what is public

When you post a request you provide details such as: a **title and description**,
**category**, **travel dates**, **party composition** (adults, children, infants,
pets), **budget**, and a **location** (an area/region, and optionally a precise
**map pin** with a search radius). A **public teaser** of your request — category,
area, dates, party size, and budget — is visible to Hosts so they can decide whether
to respond. **The teaser does not reveal your identity or contact details.**

### 2. What is disclosed, and when

- Your **name, contact details (email and phone), and full brief are NOT public.**
- A **Host sees them only after that Host chooses to "unlock" your request** (which
  costs the Host a credit). **Any Host on the Platform may choose to unlock your
  request**, at which point your name, contact details, and full brief are disclosed
  **to that Host** so they can quote you. `[COUNSEL: confirm this disclosure model and
  the consent wording shown to the Guest before posting — POPIA transparency and
  consent. Consider a clear pre-post consent checkbox.]`
- You can make a request **private/targeted to specific Hosts** you choose (rather
  than public) if you prefer to control who can respond.

### 3. How Hosts contact you

All contact happens **inside Wielo** — you receive quotes and messages in your Wielo
inbox and notifications. Hosts respond by sending a **quote**; you can compare quotes
and, if you accept one, it becomes a Booking with that Host. You are never obliged to
accept any quote.

### 4. No guarantee of a response

Posting a request **does not guarantee that any Host will respond**, that you will
receive a quote, or that a suitable option exists. Hosts choose whether to respond.

### 5. Accuracy and Wielo's role

**Wielo is a facilitator, not a party** to any resulting booking. **Quotes — their
prices, inclusions, availability, and terms — are set entirely by the Host, and
Wielo does not verify or guarantee them.** The agreement for any stay or experience
is directly between you and the Host, under the Host's own policies and our
[Terms & Conditions](/terms).

### 6. Your request expires

Requests expire automatically (typically 7 days after your travel window, or after
about 30 days). You can edit or remove your request at any time, and exercise your
**POPIA** rights via **privacy@wielo.co.za**.

## Part 2 — For Hosts responding

### 7. Unlocking a lead

- A public request shows a **teaser** for free. To see the **Guest's name, contact
  details, and full brief**, you **unlock** the request, which costs **1 credit**.
  Sending a quote may cost a further credit, except where already unlocked — so it is
  **roughly one credit per lead**. Hosts on an unlimited allowance are not charged.
- **Credits are paid for in advance and do not expire.** A credit spent to **unlock**
  a lead is charged **even if the Guest never responds and even if you never send a
  quote** — unlocking is what you pay for. A credit spent to **send a quote** is
  **refunded if that quote expires unaccepted**. `[COUNSEL: confirm this consumer/
  billing disclosure on the Host side, and refund/non-refund treatment of credits.]`

### 8. How you must treat Guest information

When you unlock a lead you receive the Guest's **personal information**. You must:

- use it **only** to respond to that specific request;
- **not** use it for unrelated marketing or share it further without a lawful basis
  and the Guest's consent;
- comply with **POPIA** as the **Responsible Party** for your own use of that
  information (Wielo is your **Operator** for information processed through the
  Platform — see Schedule A of the Terms).

### 9. Honest quoting

Quotes you send must be **accurate and honestly capable of being honoured**
(price, inclusions, availability, and terms). Misrepresentation may lead to removal
of the response, credit consequences, and account action under the Terms.

### 10. Wielo's role and monetisation

Wielo provides the lead, the quote tools, and the in-platform messaging. **Wielo
takes no commission on any resulting booking** — the booking is between you and the
Guest. Wielo's charge here is the **credit** you spend to unlock/quote.
