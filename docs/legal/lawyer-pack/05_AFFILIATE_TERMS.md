## 3. Attribution

- You have **one permanent default referral link** that never expires and earns the
  **standard rate**. If you opt into a **competition**, you also get a separate
  competition link stamped with that competition's rate.
- A referral is attributed to you by a **90-day cookie** set on the visitor's first
  click. **First touch wins:** the first valid click inside the window binds the
  referral to you permanently, and later clicks (yours or another Partner's) do not
  displace it. Re-clicking does not restart the 90-day clock.
  `[COUNSEL/CONFIG: confirm 90 days — see cover note conflict #3.]`
- **The rate is fixed at the moment of the click ("snapshot"), not at signup**, and
  is stored on the referral permanently. Editing a rate later, or a competition
  ending, does **not** change referrals already bound.
- If a competition link is used after that competition has ended, it binds at the
  **standard rate** and scores no competition points; it never downgrades an
  existing valid attribution.
- A manual **"Referred by"** field at signup (cookie-prefilled, or a Partner code
  entered by hand) is a fallback where no cookie is present.
- Wielo's recorded attribution governs. Wielo may correct or reassign an
  attribution on request within **30 days** of the bind; all such changes are
  logged.

## 4. Commission

- **Commission is earned only when a referred Host actually *pays* Wielo.** A free
  sign-up or registration earns nothing.
- **Standard rate: 25% (lifetime).** Referrals through a qualifying **Founding
  Race** competition link earn **60% (lifetime)** — see the Competition Rules.
  "Lifetime" means you earn on each successful payment for as long as that Host
  remains a paying subscriber; there is no cap and no guaranteed number of periods.
- Commission is calculated on the **subscription value the referred Host pays**
  (including multi-listing add-ons on that subscription), **exclusive of VAT**.
  Wielo is **not currently VAT-registered**. `[COUNSEL: confirm VAT — cover note #6.]`
- **Standard-rate referrals may attract an additional loyalty/tier bonus** (a
  percentage uplift once your lifetime cleared earnings pass a threshold set by
  Wielo). This bonus **does not** apply to Founding Race (60%) referrals. Thresholds
  and rates are admin-set and may change prospectively. `[COUNSEL: confirm whether to

- Commission **accrues** when a referred payment succeeds and is **held** until the
  refund window passes, after which it becomes **cleared** and payable.

## 5. Refunds and reversals (no lapse clawback)

- **There is no clawback when a referred Host merely lapses, cancels, or
  downgrades** — you simply earn nothing further from that Host until (and unless)
  they pay again.
- The **only** reversal is a **refund, chargeback, or reversal of a specific
  payment**. Commission on a refunded payment is voided. If it had already been
  paid to you, the amount is **debited from your balance and carried forward** until
  future earnings absorb it; Wielo does not otherwise pursue cash recovery.

## 6. Payouts

- Commission on a payment becomes **payable after the refund window** (currently on
  **day 33** after the payment clears). **No commission is paid on a payment
  refunded within that window.**
- Payouts are made by **EFT** to your verified account, and typically arrive within
  **7 business days** of the payable date.
- The **minimum payout balance is R1,000**; balances below it roll forward.
  Sweep-up exceptions (any balance paid regardless of amount) apply on **competition
  close**, on **account closure**, and **once annually in June**.

Wielo may run time-bound **Campaigns** with leaderboards, milestones, and **cash

## 9. Abuse, suspension, and forfeiture

Wielo may suspend or remove an Affiliate for breach, fraud, or abuse (including fake
accounts, self-referral, brand-term bidding, or false feature/earnings claims).
Doing so may **void pending commission, forfeit competition standing and prizes,
and reverse commission generated through prohibited conduct**. Wielo verifies all
activations and **its decision on qualification is final**.

## 10. Data protection

Hosts sign up **directly with Wielo**; Partners do not handle Host personal
information as part of the programme. Your own processing of any personal
information in your promotion must comply with **POPIA**.

## 11. Changes and termination

Wielo may update these terms or the programme's rates and figures **prospectively**;
continued participation after a change is acceptance, and **rates already snapshotted
onto existing referrals do not change**. Either party may end participation at any
time; cleared, non-reversed commission earned before termination remains payable
subject to the threshold and these terms.
