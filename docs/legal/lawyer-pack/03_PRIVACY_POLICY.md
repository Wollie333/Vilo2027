This policy explains what personal information Wielo collects, why, how we use and
protect it, who we share it with, and your rights under the **Protection of
Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA)**. It covers our website, host websites
we power, mobile apps, and services (the “**Platform**”).

## 1. Who this policy covers

- **Hosts** — people and businesses that list on the Platform.
- **Guests** — people who book, enquire, post a Looking-For request, or leave a
  review (including **guests without a Wielo account**, whose contact details a Host
  may capture to complete a Booking).
- **Affiliates/Partners** and general visitors.

Where a **Host** collects a Guest's personal information to fulfil a Booking, the
**Host is the Responsible Party** for that information and **Wielo acts as the
Host's Operator** (see Schedule A of the [Terms & Conditions](/terms)). **This
policy governs Wielo's own processing.**

## 2. Personal information we collect

**You give us directly:**
- **Account & profile:** name, email address, phone number, password, role, country,
  avatar, bio, languages, preferred cities, and your marketing-consent choice.
- **Host details:** business/display name, listings content, social links, brochure,
  and **banking/payout details** (stored encrypted) and tax details.
- **Bookings & enquiries:** guest name, email, phone, special requests, party
  details, dates, and the policy/terms versions you accepted — **captured even for
  guests who do not create an account**.
- **Payments:** amount, currency, method, and the provider's transaction reference
  and response. **We do not receive or store full card numbers** (see clause 6).
- **Content you post:** reviews and review photos, Looking-For requests, quotes,
  support messages, and site content.

**We collect automatically:**
- **Device & connection data** and usage/log data. Where we log a visitor's IP for
  security or attribution, we store a **hashed fingerprint (SHA-256), not a raw IP
  address**. Precise IP is used only transiently for bot-protection and (where
  enabled) ad measurement.
- **Precise geolocation (latitude/longitude)** where you set a map pin on a
  Looking-For request (optional — see the [Looking-For Notice](/legal/looking-for-notice)).
- **Cookies and similar technologies** — see the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).
- **Mobile push tokens** if you enable notifications in the app.

**From third parties, where authorised:**
- Payment confirmations and status from **Paystack** and **PayPal**.
- Where you connect them, imported reviews and page data from external platforms
  (e.g. a Host's Facebook page).

We do not intentionally collect **special personal information** or children's
information — please do not submit it. `[COUNSEL: confirm no special-PI processing

## 3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis (POPIA)

We process personal information to:

- create and manage your account and provide the Platform;
- process Bookings, Subscriptions, and payments, and produce receipts/invoices;
- enable Host–Guest communication and Booking fulfilment;
- run the **Looking-For** feature and deliver quotes;
- collect, verify, publish, and moderate **reviews**;
- operate the **Affiliate Program** and competitions (attribution, commission,
  prizes);
- send **transactional** messages (confirmations, receipts, security and account
  notices);
- prevent, detect, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
- provide support and resolve disputes;
- comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations; and
- **with your consent**, send product updates and marketing, and enable optional
  analytics and advertising measurement.

Our POPIA lawful bases (justifications) include **performance of a contract** with
you, **compliance with a legal obligation**, our **legitimate interests** (e.g.
security, fraud prevention, and improving the Platform) balanced against your
rights, and your **consent** where required (e.g. marketing and non-essential
cookies/pixels). **Marketing consent is off by default** and recorded, and you can
withdraw it at any time. `[COUNSEL: confirm the basis mapping per processing
activity.]`

## 4. Reviews

When you leave a review, we collect the review content, ratings, and any photos and
associate them with the relevant **completed Booking** to confirm the review is
genuine. Published reviews show your display name and are visible publicly. How
reviews are collected, verified, published, moderated, and aggregated — and how
Hosts may record private conduct ratings of Guests — is set out in the
[Reviews Disclosure](/legal/review-disclosure). `[COUNSEL: define the lawful basis
and transparency for host-to-guest conduct ratings — see cover note conflict #8.]`

## 5. Looking-For requests

When you post a Looking-For request, we process the details you share so Hosts can
respond. A **public teaser** (category, area, dates, party size, budget) is visible
to Hosts; your **name, contact details, and full brief are disclosed to a Host only
after that Host chooses to unlock your request**. The specific notice, including
what is exposed and to whom, is the [Looking-For Notice](/legal/looking-for-notice).

## 6. Payments and card data

Wielo is **not the merchant of record for Bookings** — Guests pay **Hosts**
directly through the Host's own **Paystack** or **PayPal** account, or by manual
EFT. Wielo's own fees (Subscriptions, add-ons) are charged to Hosts. In all cases,
**card details are entered on the payment provider's secure page and are not
received, processed, or stored on Wielo's systems.** We retain only the provider's
transaction reference and response, and (for EFT) any proof-of-payment you upload.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable, appropriate technical and organisational measures (**POPIA §19**),
including encryption in transit, **encrypted storage of sensitive fields** (such as
banking details), hashing of visitor fingerprints, strict **role-based access
controls**, verification of payment webhooks, and regular security review. No system
is perfectly secure; please protect your credentials and report concerns to
**privacy@wielo.co.za**. If a security compromise affects your personal information,
we will notify you and the Information Regulator as **POPIA §22** requires.

## 10. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and for a
reasonable period afterwards to satisfy legal, tax, accounting, and
dispute-resolution requirements, after which it is deleted or de-identified.
Certain records (for example financial, audit, and consent records) are retained
for the periods the law requires. Non-converting accounts are **restricted, not
deleted**, and remain exportable. `[COUNSEL: set concrete retention periods per data

## 11. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you;
- **correct or update** inaccurate or incomplete information;
- **delete** your information or **object** to processing, where the law allows;
- **withdraw consent** for processing based on consent (e.g. marketing);
- **not** be subject to unsolicited electronic marketing; and
- **complain** to the Information Regulator.

You can request **data export or deletion** from your account settings; we log and
process these requests, including for **guests without an account** who contact us.
Some information may be retained where the law requires it even after a deletion
request. To exercise any right, contact **privacy@wielo.co.za**.

## 12. Children

The Platform is not intended for people under **18**. We do not knowingly collect
information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact
**privacy@wielo.co.za** and we will delete it.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated by email or in-app
notice. The “Last updated” date reflects the current version, and the version in
force when you make a Booking is recorded against that Booking.
