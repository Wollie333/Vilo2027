Wielo believes reviews should be **honest, verified, and un-bought**. This
disclosure explains how guest reviews are collected, verified, published,
moderated, and aggregated on the Platform, so Guests and Hosts know exactly how the
system works.

## 1. Who can leave a review — verified stays only

- A review can be left **only by the Guest of a real Booking that has been
  completed** (the stay has ended and check-out is recorded). There is **no way to
  post a review without a completed Booking** — the system will not accept one.
- After check-out, we send the Guest a **secure, single-use review link** (valid for
  **30 days**). A Guest without a Wielo account can still review through that link;
  their display name comes from the Booking.
- **One review per Booking.** A Host may also manually invite a review for their own
  completed, paid, un-reviewed Bookings. Invitations are **neutral nudges** — see
  clause 5.

## 2. What a review contains

- A required **overall rating** (1–5 stars).
- Optional **written feedback** (up to 2,000 characters).
- Optional **category ratings** (1–5) for cleanliness, communication, check-in,
  accuracy, location, and value.
- An optional **trip type** (e.g. couple, family, solo, friends, business).
- Up to **six photos** uploaded by the Guest.

## 3. How reviews are published and moderated

- Reviews are **published immediately** on submission — there is **no
  pre-publication moderation queue**. `[COUNSEL: confirm the disclosure adequately
  warns readers that content is user-generated and not pre-screened.]`
- Once submitted, a review's rating and content are **locked and cannot be edited or
  deleted by the Guest or the Host** (only a platform super-administrator can change
  a published state).
- A **Host may publicly reply** to a review, and may **pin one review** to feature it,
  but **cannot alter, hide, or remove** a review.
- A **Host may flag** a review for review by Wielo (for example: false information, a
  personal attack, or a booking that never occurred). Flagging alerts Wielo; it does
  not remove the review.
- **Wielo (administrators) may hide or restore** a review after review, and these
  actions are audit-logged. Wielo does **not** routinely pre-screen reviews and is
  not responsible for user-generated content, but will act on unlawful content.

## 4. How reviews are displayed and aggregated

- Published, non-hidden reviews are **visible to the public**, including on the
  relevant property page, and show the Guest's **display name**.
- A property's and a Host's **average star rating** and **review count** are
  automatically calculated from published reviews and **update when a review is
  added or hidden**.
- The public may mark a review as **"helpful."**

## 5. No incentives — reviews are not bought

**Wielo does not offer any discount, voucher, credit, free night, or other reward
for leaving a review, or for leaving a positive one.** Review invitations are
neutral. Hosts and Guests **must not** offer, solicit, or accept incentives for
reviews, and must not post false, incentivised, or manipulated reviews. This lets us
describe Wielo reviews as **verified and un-incentivised**. `[COUNSEL: confirm this
supports an "authentic/verified reviews" claim under the CPA and ASA/ARB code.]`

## 6. Imported (external) reviews

Hosts may **import reviews from external platforms** (for example, a linked
Facebook page). Where displayed, these are **third-party-authored** reviews
republished on the Platform and are **labelled as external**. Wielo does not verify
the underlying stay for imported reviews. `[COUNSEL: confirm labelling/consent for
republished third-party reviews.]`

## 7. Host conduct ratings of Guests (transparency notice)

Separately from public reviews, **Hosts can record private conduct ratings about
Guests** (covering matters such as payment, communication, and adherence to house
rules) after a completed or no-show Booking. These ratings form a **shared
reputation signal visible to other Hosts** to protect platform integrity. **Guests
do not see these ratings.** Because this processes a Guest's personal information,
Wielo will provide a lawful basis and honour data-subject rights under **POPIA**, and

## 8. Reporting a review

To report a review you believe is unlawful, defamatory, or false, Hosts can use the
in-app **flag** tool; anyone can contact **support@wielo.co.za**. We will review
reports and may hide content that breaches our Terms or the law.
