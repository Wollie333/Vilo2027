This policy explains what personal information Wielo collects, why, how we use and
protect it, and your rights under the **Protection of Personal Information Act,
2013 (POPIA)**. It covers our website, apps, and services (the “Platform”), and
absorbs our cookies notice and our notices for reviews and Looking-For requests.

## 1. Who this policy covers

- **Hosts** — people and businesses that list on the Platform.
- **Guests** — people who book, enquire, or post a Looking-For request.
- **Affiliates** and other visitors.

Where a **Host** collects a Guest's personal information to fulfil a Booking, the
Host is the **Responsible Party** for that information and Wielo acts as the
Host's **Operator** (see Schedule A of the Terms of Service). This policy governs
Wielo's own processing.

## 2. Information we collect

**You give us directly:**
- Account details (name, email, phone, password, role).
- Host details (business name, listings content, banking/payout details, tax
  info).
- Booking and enquiry details, messages exchanged through the Platform.
- Payment information you enter with our payment providers.
- Content you post — reviews, Looking-For requests, support messages.

**We collect automatically:**
- Device and connection data (IP address, browser, device identifiers), log data,
  and usage data.
- Cookies and similar technologies (see clause 7).

**From third parties, where authorised:**
- Confirmations and status from payment providers (Paystack, PayPal).
- Any identity or verification data you authorise a provider to share.

We do not intentionally collect **special personal information** or children's
information; do not submit it. `[COUNSEL: confirm no special-PI processing occurs
via any feature.]`

## 3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We process personal information to:

- create and manage your account and provide the Platform;
- process Bookings, Subscriptions, and payments;
- enable Host–Guest communication and Booking fulfilment;
- send **transactional** messages (booking confirmations, receipts, security and
  account notices);
- prevent, detect, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
- provide support and resolve disputes;
- comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations; and
- with your consent, send product updates and marketing, and enable optional
  analytics.

Our POPIA lawful bases include performance of a contract with you, compliance
with a legal obligation, our legitimate interests (e.g. security and service
improvement) balanced against your rights, and your consent where required (e.g.
marketing and non-essential cookies). `[COUNSEL: confirm the basis mapping.]`

## 4. Reviews

When you leave a review, we collect the review content and associate it with your
account and the relevant Booking to verify it is genuine. Published reviews show
your display name and may be visible publicly. How we collect, verify, and display
reviews is described at [/legal/review-disclosure](/legal/review-disclosure).

## 5. Looking-For requests

When you post a Looking-For request, we process the details you share (such as
destination, dates, party size, budget, and contact preference) so Hosts can
respond. Only what is necessary is shared with Hosts who may fulfil the request.
The specific notice is at [/legal/looking-for-notice](/legal/looking-for-notice).

## 6. Sharing and disclosure

We share personal information:

- **Between Host and Guest** as needed to complete and manage a Booking.
- With **payment providers** — Paystack and PayPal — to process payments.
- With **service providers** who process on our behalf under contract —
  infrastructure and database hosting (Supabase), email delivery (Resend), and
  similar operators.
- With **regulators, law enforcement, or courts** where legally required, and to
  protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Platform.
- In a **business transfer** (merger, acquisition, restructure), subject to this
  policy.

**We do not sell personal information**, and we do not use third-party
advertising or behavioural-targeting cookies.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

- **Strictly necessary cookies** keep you signed in, maintain your session,
  remember your locale and currency, and protect against fraud and abuse. These
  cannot be switched off without breaking core functionality.
- **Analytics** (when enabled) is **opt-in**, first-party, and runs with IP-
  address anonymisation. No analytics cookies are set before you consent.
- **Payment providers** (Paystack, PayPal) may set their own cookies on payment
  pages under their own policies.

You can manage non-essential cookies from the cookie banner shown on your first
visit, or later in your settings, and you can clear cookies in your browser
(which will sign you out and reset preferences).

(payment, email, infrastructure) process personal information in other countries.
POPIA §72 permits these cross-border transfers because the recipients are subject
to laws or binding agreements upholding principles substantially similar to
POPIA, and our contracts require a comparable standard of protection.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable, appropriate technical and organisational measures, including
encryption in transit, encrypted storage of sensitive fields (such as banking
details), strict role-based access controls, and regular security review. No
system is perfectly secure; we ask you to protect your credentials and report
concerns to privacy@wielo.co.za.

## 10. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and for a
reasonable period afterwards to satisfy legal, tax, accounting, and dispute-
resolution requirements, after which it is deleted or de-identified. Certain
records (for example financial and audit records) are retained for the periods
the law requires. `[COUNSEL: set concrete retention periods per data category.]`

## 11. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you;
- **correct or update** inaccurate or incomplete information;
- **delete** your information or **object** to processing, where the law allows;
- **withdraw consent** for processing based on consent (e.g. marketing);
- **not** be subject to unsolicited electronic marketing; and
- **complain** to the Information Regulator.

You can request **data export or deletion** from your account settings; we log and
process these requests. To exercise any right, contact **privacy@wielo.co.za**.
Some information may be retained where the law requires it even after a deletion
request.

## 12. Children

The Platform is not intended for people under **18**. We do not knowingly collect
information from children. If you believe a child has given us information,
contact privacy@wielo.co.za and we will delete it.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated by email or in-
app notice. The “Last updated” date reflects the current version, and the version
in force when you make a Booking is recorded against that Booking.
